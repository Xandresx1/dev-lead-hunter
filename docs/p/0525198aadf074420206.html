The final system must be ready for real customer orders. ## Customer Experience Inside WhatsApp The customer should be able to: 1. Select products, quantities, sizes, add-ons, removals, and special instructions. 4. review and edit the shopping cart before confirming the order. 5. Automatically receive a calculation containing: - Product prices. - Add-ons. - Delivery fee. - Value-added tax at a configurable rate. - Final total. 6.
