Build Supabase B2B Sales Platform The app we want to build A B2B prospecting and sales management platform built on Supabase. We want to organise healthcare companies and contacts, prepare campaigns for each market, and turn replies into sales opportunities with a clear owner and follow-up. La Pastilla will be the first team to use the app. A complete record for each company Distributors, manufacturers and end customers, with their contacts, countries, represented brands, documents and history. Include data imports and detection of potential duplicates. Find the right audience Filter by country, sector, language, owner and interest. Save segments and review each campaign’s recipients, applying exclusions and unsubscribe requests. Manage opportunities as a team Link emails, tasks and opportunities. Show the stage, owner and next action, with dashboards for activity and results by market. Use the prototype as the design reference Keep the prototype’s visual identity and navigation. The app will provide shared data, access for multiple users, and a Spanish and English interface for desktop and mobile. MEDCOMBO · Development brief · 18 November 2026 How it will be built with Supabase Supabase will be the technical foundation. The developer will build the interface, business rules and connections to external services. Data and automation PostgreSQL will store companies, people, segments, campaigns, messages, opportunities and tasks. Edge Functions will run server operations; Cron and Queues will organise scheduled and pending jobs. Users and private files Supabase Auth will manage sign-in. Row Level Security (RLS) policies will restrict records by organisation, role and assignment. Storage will hold private files; external service credentials will remain on the server. Integrations the developer must build Gmail: link conversations and capture replies. Sales email: schedule delivery with sending limits, unsubscribe handling and duplicate prevention. Odoo: exchange contacts and sales references. AI: summarise and classify enquiries, and suggest owners or draft replies. The web interface will be hosted separately. Email delivery and AI will use external services with separate costs. La Pastilla will control the Supabase account and source code. The prototype will be the reference for agreeing the screens. First release An app connected to Supabase: sign-in, permissions, companies, contacts, records, imports, search, filters and private documents. The team must be able to work with the same shared information. Second release Segments, templates, scheduled campaigns, Gmail integration, reply tracking, opportunities and tasks.
