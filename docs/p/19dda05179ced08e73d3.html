Spanish Voice Recording Recruitment We’re building a high-quality Spanish speech dataset and are looking for native speakers and recruiters in Mexico, Peru, and Costa Rica. It’s simple, remote, and done from your smartphone. PROJECT OVERVIEW Activity: Read 450 short sentences aloud using a designated mobile recording app Text: Provided in App Countries: Mexico, Peru, and Costa Rica (200+ participants per country) Rate: USD 12.50 per completed participant SCOPE OF WORK Install the recording app and record the provided sentences in a clear, natural voice Complete the full set of recordings Recruiters and teams: source participants, support their app setup, and ensure each participant completes their recordings Each participant may take part only once PARTICIPANT REQUIREMENTS Native Spanish speakers residing in Mexico, Peru, or Costa Rica A working smartphone and a quiet place to record A balanced mix of male and female participants across age groups is required WHO CAN APPLY Individuals who meet the requirements above Data collection companies or agencies with established participant networks Community leaders, group organizers, or local coordinators in any of the three countries PARTICIPATION ELIGIBILITY Participants who have already contributed to this project through any other team, particularly within the past 7–10 days, are not eligible. This applies to the following tasks: Mexico: D000317 Peru: D000318 Costa Rica: D000319 The platform’s quality system detects repeat contributions automatically, and duplicate recordings are not eligible for compensation. COMPENSATION TERMS (per participant) Milestone 1: USD 5, funded at project start Milestone 2: USD 7.50 remaining balance Payment is released only after the recordings are accepted by the DataPlus Quality Control team Recording approval is decided entirely by DataPlus QC, not by our team, and we cannot release payment before their acceptance The QC review usually takes around 10–14 working days, though it may be shorter or longer depending on review volume. Please apply only if you are comfortable with this timeline. HOW TO APPLY Please include in your proposal: Your country and city Whether you are applying as an individual or a recruiter/team For recruiters: the number of participants you can provide per country Full project details and app setup instructions will be shared with shortlisted applicants.
