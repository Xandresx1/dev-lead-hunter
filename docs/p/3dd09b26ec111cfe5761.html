Build Searchable Journal Archive Platform Functionality to Parse and Display Journals on Our Website My stamp club produces a quarterly journal. The Journal is posted on our website with both the ability to browse the complete journal and search the journal. The journal search is more than just a key word search, it pulls up the complete articles with the key word being searched irrespective of the page. For example, if the article is on page 2-4 and then continues to page 10 and 11, the search will display the complete article. Similar to how a story may be organized in a Newspaper. The current functionality relies upon the individual who runs the program breaking down the content, which has not been reliable. The club is looking to develop functionality that replaces and enhances this functionality and is owned by the club so we can use this functionality on other journals. The current functionality can be found on: https://www.collectorsclub.org/ccp/browse-search-the-ccp/ Test copies of the journal (CCP) can be obtained by using the browse selection.
