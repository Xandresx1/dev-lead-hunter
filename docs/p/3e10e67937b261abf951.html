AI Email Support Auto-Responder I want to take the routine questions that flood our support inbox and let an AI handle them automatically. The first channel in scope is our main email inbox; live-chat and social media will follow later, but for now everything happens in email. All the reference material you need already exists inside our past support threads and the saved reply templates the team has been polishing for years. The job is to ingest that content, build a reliable knowledge base from it, and wire an AI model (GPT-4, Claude, or a comparable LLM) so it can draft and send replies without human intervention whenever the request matches one of those common patterns. When the AI is not confident, it should leave the message flagged and routed to the right human queue instead of guessing. I expect a lightweight workflow—Zapier, Make, a direct Gmail API script, or a Help Scout/Freshdesk app—whatever you’re most comfortable with, as long as it can run securely in our environment and is easy for the team to tweak later. Deliverables • Cleaned and structured knowledge base built from the exported email threads and templates • Deployed AI responder connected to the support inbox, set to auto-send only when confidence exceeds the agreed threshold • Fallback routing or labeling for anything outside that threshold • Short loom/video walkthrough plus concise setup documentation so the team can maintain it Acceptance Criteria • At least 80 % of the top 50 recurring questions are answered automatically during the pilot week • Zero incorrect answers in the sampled pilot set; low-confidence cases must be routed, not sent • All code, prompts, and workflow diagrams handed over in our private repo Once this foundation is solid, we’ll talk about extending the same system to live chat and social DMs, but success here means happy customers who get an instant, accurate reply in their inbox.
