RAG Development Project – Request for Indicative Quotation We're looking for an experienced AI/RAG developer or white-label development agency to help deliver a knowledge retrieval and AI-assisted response generation solution for a UK B2B organisation. The system will support an internal technical team by retrieving relevant information from historical customer support records and technical documentation, then generating evidence-based draft responses for human review. We will manage the client relationship and Salesforce integration. We're primarily looking for a specialist to design, develop and validate the **RAG and AI components**. ### Expected scope - Ingest and index historical Salesforce Cases and email correspondence, PDFs, Word documents, technical diagrams, images and potentially videos. - Develop automated ingestion and synchronisation processes for new, updated and deleted content. - Configure semantic/keyword retrieval, ranking and source referencing. - Integrate a commercially available LLM to generate technically accurate draft responses with citations and appropriate handling of uncertainty. - Provide APIs for integration with Salesforce and support ongoing maintenance. ### Proposed milestones and deliverables 1. **Architecture and evaluation framework:** Agree technology, representative test data, benchmark enquiries and measurable acceptance criteria. 2. **Ingestion proof of concept:** Demonstrate accurate extraction, chunking, metadata and indexing across representative document types. 3. **Retrieval validation:** Demonstrate that relevant, authoritative evidence is consistently retrieved and selected. 4. **Scaled testing:** Validate retrieval performance against a substantially larger knowledge base. 5. **Response generation:** Deliver accurate, evidence-based draft responses with traceable citations. 6. **Production readiness:** Deliver documented APIs, automated synchronisation, monitoring, deployment and technical handover. These milestones are suggestions, not fixed requirements. **We welcome challenges to the proposed approach and recommendations for a more effective or cost-efficient delivery model.** ### Information requested At this stage, we're looking for: - An indicative cost range and estimated development effort. - An outline of your recommended architecture and technologies. - Relevant experience delivering production RAG systems, particularly with technical documentation. - Whether you would use managed services or reusable components to reduce development time. - An estimated delivery timeframe and earliest availability. - Expected ongoing infrastructure, AI usage and maintenance costs. - **What additional information you would need from us to provide a comprehensive technical proposal and quotation.** An initial working MVP is ideally required by **mid-January 2027**, although we recognise that scope may need to be prioritised to achieve this.
