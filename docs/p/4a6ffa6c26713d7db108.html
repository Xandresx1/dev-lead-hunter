Build Secure K–12 Case Management SaaS ResolvED HHB — School Case Management and Restorative Practices Software PROJECT OVERVIEW I am seeking an experienced full-stack software developer or small team to build ResolvED HHB, a secure, cloud-based SaaS application for K–12 schools and districts. HHB stands for Harassment, Hazing, and Bullying. The platform will initially serve Vermont schools, with the potential for national expansion. The software will combine regulatory compliance, investigation management, student support, and restorative practices into one intuitive system. I am an experienced educational leader with more than 20 years in school leadership and restorative practices. I have developed the product vision and initial workflows and need a technical partner to create a functional, scalable commercial product. THE PROBLEM School administrators must navigate complicated legal requirements when responding to harassment, hazing, and bullying complaints. Responsibilities include investigating allegations, documenting evidence, meeting deadlines, communicating with families, determining findings, supporting students, and maintaining records. Much of this work currently relies on disconnected documents, spreadsheets, and manual tracking. ResolvED HHB will simplify these processes while emphasizing accountability, student well-being, relationship repair, and prevention. CORE FEATURES 1. INCIDENT REPORTING AND CASE MANAGEMENT Secure incident intake forms capturing allegations, involved individuals, witnesses, dates, supporting evidence, and immediate actions. Automatic case identification numbers, searchable records, and chronological activity histories. 2. COMPLIANCE AND GUIDED WORKFLOWS Configurable workflows aligned with Vermont HHB laws and school policies. Guided procedural steps, deadlines, reminders, required documentation, and notification tracking. Ability to update rules as regulations change and eventually support additional states. 3. INVESTIGATION MANAGEMENT Structured investigation workspace supporting interview notes, witness statements, confidential evidence uploads, timelines, documentation of allegations and responses, findings, approvals, and investigation report generation. 4. RESTORATIVE PRACTICES AND STUDENT SUPPORT This is a distinguishing feature of ResolvED HHB. The platform should provide restorative questions, guided reflection, student support planning, relationship-repair strategies, restorative meeting preparation, agreements, reentry planning, and follow-up monitoring. Restorative processes must remain separate from legally required investigations, with appropriate safeguards and voluntary participation. 5.
