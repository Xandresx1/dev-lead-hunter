Moving Company Job Platform I want to build a mobile-first application that lets moving company owners run every step of a move from their phone. The owners are the primary users, and the app needs to give them one place to create new jobs, receive customer booking requests in real time, assign those jobs to available movers, and then follow the crew’s progress until the work is complete. Alongside the owner interface, customers must be able to submit their move details so jobs flow straight into the system, while self-employed movers should see only the jobs assigned to them and update status as they work.
