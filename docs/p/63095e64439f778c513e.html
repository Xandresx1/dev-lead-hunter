Ontario Residential BCA Report Preparation We are looking for a reliable freelancer to assist with preparing Building Condition Assessment (BCA) reports for residential properties across Ontario, Canada. Scope of Work: Review and organize site inspection photographs provided by our team. Identify common residential building materials and components, including foundations, roofing, exterior siding (vinyl, brick, stucco, etc.), windows, doors, flooring, ceilings, walls, and interior finishes. Prepare clear, professional descriptions of building components based on photographs and information provided. Perform approximate quantity takeoffs, including the number of windows and doors, flooring areas, ceiling areas, wall finishes, roofing areas, and other building components. Enter descriptions, quantities, and photographs into our designated BCA reporting application. Use AI tools to assist with drafting descriptions and improving report preparation efficiency, while ensuring technical accuracy. Organize photographs under the appropriate building components and report sections. Requirements: Basic knowledge of residential construction methods and building materials commonly used in Ontario, Canada. Ability to identify building materials and components from photographs. Experience with building assessments, construction estimating, quantity takeoffs, or technical report preparation is preferred. Good English writing and attention to detail. Ability to work independently using our reporting application. What We Provide: Site inspection photographs and available property information. Access to our BCA reporting application. Report templates and instructions. Guidance and examples of previously completed reports.
