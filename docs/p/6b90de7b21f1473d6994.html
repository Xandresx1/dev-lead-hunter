Peer-to-Peer Rental App MVP I’m ready to build the very first version of RentIt as a mobile app that serves both renters and equipment owners. For this initial release, I want the experience to feel effortless and trustworthy, so your priority will be rock-solid secure payments, refundable deposits, and a transparent user-review system. Design should follow Material or Cupertino guidelines so it feels native on both platforms.
