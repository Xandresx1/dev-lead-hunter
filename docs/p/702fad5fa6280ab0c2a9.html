Full-Stack Developer for Dating Platform Aphrodite is a dating platform only for students of the our pilot university. It deliberately does the opposite of Tinder, Bumble and Hinge: 1) no swiping and no endless catalogue. Students get a few curated matches ("drops") twice a week. 2) Skip-to-offline: When two students are both interested, the app offers 3 date slots at partner venues on or near campus. One shared choice books the date. 3) Desired Metric: time-to-offline, not screen time. What you will build (MVP scope) 1. Sign-up restricted to campus identity provider: only university addresses, with email verification. 2. Profile card (signals over photos): name, age (16–99), study program (fixed catalogue of ~29 Bachelor, Master and PhD programs), gender, interested in, "Looking for", 3–5 values and 3–10 interests from fixed lists, career aspirations (max 500 characters). Guided onboarding wizard plus an edit screen. 3. Matching drop: an algorithm scores pairs on study program and on the overlap of values and interests, with configurable weights. Drops run on a manual trigger for the MVP. Students aged 16–17 are only matched with each other, never with adults. 4. Date booking: on mutual interest, both students see 3 slots (date, time, partner venue). The first shared choice books the date into an in-app calendar. 5. Presence check: a bilateral QR scan during the booked slot confirms that both showed up. No location tracking. 6. No-show and cancellation policy: a no-show triggers a 1–2 week lockout. Cancelling more than 6 h ahead is free twice per semester; the third cancellation triggers a lockout. Cancelling less than 6 h ahead counts as a no-show. 7. Semester-end deletion of personal data. 8. Mobile-first web app (PWA-ready), accessible (WCAG AA), light and dark mode. Expected tech stack We have a preferred stack.
