Kids Playcenter Event App I’m building an all-in-one application that lets our playcenter team craft a personalized “birthday show” for every party and instantly hand the family a downloadable keepsake video. Core flow • From any PC, tablet or phone, the operator creates a new event, types the child’s name and age, then drags in photos from birth to today and short video greetings supplied by friends or relatives. • The system stitches those assets with themed animations (boys, girls or unisex), a 5-to-1 visual countdown, an upbeat birthday song and AI-generated cameo wishes from famous athletes, artists or even politicians. • During the party we can still pop in extra photos or clips on the fly; once the show ends, the final montage renders on the server and the family receives a link or QR code to download it. Multi-platform expectations The operator interface must work smoothly on iOS, Android and any modern browser. Guests scan a large screen QR code to read about the arena, redeem offers or fetch their video later.
