Android Classroom Control Software I need a custom software solution that lets me supervise and assist students during live online lessons by interacting directly with their Android devices. The core idea is simple: from a teacher-side dashboard I should be able to see every connected student screen in real time, pop any of them out to full view, and—when permission is granted—take touch-level control to demonstrate a step or fix an issue. I’m flexible on how students join the session: a class code, scan-link, or a small companion app are all acceptable as long as setup stays hassle-free and never requires rooting the device. Performance must stay smooth for typical class sizes of 20–30 learners, and everything needs to respect Google Play policies and standard Android security rules.
