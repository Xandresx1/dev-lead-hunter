Build AI Sales Call Automation Platform We want to build and own a service that calls our Sales team as soon as a vehicle enquiry comes in, connects an agent to the customer and shows managers which leads were missed, which need a callback and how well the team handled each call. How the system should work 1. When an Auto Trader enquiry comes in, our existing Leads feed continues as normal. The new service receives a copy by email or approved API, reads the customer’s phone number, vehicle and enquiry. 2. The service calls our direct number, which rings the Sales hunt group. When someone answers, they hear a short summary of the enquiry and press 1 to accept. Only then does the service dial the customer and connect both calls. 3. The system records what happened. We need to distinguish no agent accepted, customer didn’t answer, voicemail reached with no message left, voicemail left, customer reached and call failed. Where the evidence is unclear, flag the outcome for review. 4. Leads needing follow-up go into a live queue with an owner and a due time. The system should respect opening hours. AI call scoring and coaching We want the system to transcribe calls and score them against criteria we can edit ourselves. The AI should listen for whether the agent answered the enquiry, asked relevant questions, offered a specific appointment where appropriate, handled objections and agreed a clear next step. Each score should include a short quote, a recording timestamp and a practical coaching point. Managers should be able to correct scores and outcomes. We’ll supply reviewed calls to calibrate the scorecard before the pilot. Dashboard and automated emails - Live callback queue showing missed drips, customer voicemails, overdue callbacks and who owns each lead. - Daily management email with separate sections for drips nobody accepted and calls that went to customer voicemail. Show whether a message was left, what action has been taken and what still needs doing. - Weekly coaching email showing response times, calls accepted, customers reached, appointments booked and coaching points by agent. - Performance dashboard with filters by date, lead source and agent. It should be possible to open a report row and see the enquiry, call history, recording, transcript and outcome where available. Who we’re looking for A senior developer who has personally built a live programmable voice system, another voice API or SIP. You should have experience connecting two calls, collecting keypad input, handling caller ID and call-status events, and working with recordings and an existing phone system. We also need experience with backend APIs, email intake, scheduled reporting and AI transcription or scoring. How we’ll start Please quote separately for a paid proof of concept using sample enquiries and test numbers.
