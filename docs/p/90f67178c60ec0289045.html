Moving Marketplace Website w/ Bubble 1. Project Overview Build a production-ready moving-services marketplace in Bubble with public pages, AI inventory recognition, automated pricing, customer offers, moving-date auctions, referral tokens, reservations, customer dashboards, email/SMS, administration, and a white-label partner program. Use shared databases and workflows for regular and partner customers. 2. Pages and Responsive Design Include homepage, About Us, Contact Us, How It Works, pricing explanation, moving-company network, guarantees, FAQs, signup entry, passwordless login, Terms & Conditions, and Privacy Policy. Build moving details, inventory review, offer submission, auction entry, leaderboard, token/referral pages, customer dashboards, booking confirmations, admin screens, partner landing pages, and a secure partner portal. All customer/admin features must work on desktop, tablet, and smartphones, including uploads, editing, bidding, reporting, and management screens. 3. Inventory and AI Import a supplied catalog of approximately 400 household items with cubic-foot values. Provide no-code admin controls for descriptions, categories, volumes, and active status. Customers select items/quantities, upload multiple photos, or provide written inventory lists. Integrate approved AI to identify items/quantities and map them to the catalog. Allow review, correction, additions/removals, and duplicate resolution before confirmation. Flag uncertain/unmatched results; do not assign arbitrary values. Prevent double-counting across submission methods. Total volume = sum of confirmed item quantity multiplied by assigned cubic feet. Preserve uploads, confirmed inventory, quantities, and calculation inputs. 4. Routing, Pricing, and Offers Collect date and pickup/delivery addresses. Calculate pickup-to-delivery-to-pickup routing and store round-trip miles and drive time. Maintain separate admin-editable marketplace and traditional moving-company rates. Each price = total cubic feet multiplied by its volume rate, plus round-trip miles multiplied by its mileage rate. Display the traditional estimate first. Keep acceptance thresholds, fulfillment costs, and margins confidential. Preserve issued pricing inputs and agreed amounts when settings change. Evaluate customer offers on the backend. Accepted offers proceed to booking and cannot be lowered or resubmitted for the unchanged request. Rejected customers may repeatedly increase their offer or explore the auction. Exploration reveals the guaranteed price and offers direct booking or official auction entry; exploration alone does not enroll them. Require a rejected offer before auction entry. Starting bid = highest rejected offer. Every move qualifies regardless of size, volume, distance, cost, profitability, or rejected amount. No additional minimum auction bid. 5.
