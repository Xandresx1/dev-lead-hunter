AI Email Support Assistant I need an AI-driven platform that reads incoming customer emails about orders, returns, or account issues and instantly drafts clear, accurate replies my support team can approve with a single click. Acceptance criteria 1. At least 90 % of test emails about standard order, return, or account questions produce a usable draft that needs minimal editing. 2. Solution deploys on my preferred cloud (AWS) with clear setup docs and hand-off. Provide a short outline of your proposed architecture, the NLP/LLM stack you’d choose, and any similar projects you’ve completed so I can gauge fit.
