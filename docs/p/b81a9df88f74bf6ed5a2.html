Service Booking Website & Admin Dashboard I run a small team that handles cleaning, moving, furniture assembly, delivery, and gardening jobs. I need a modern, mobile-friendly website that makes it effortless for visitors to land on the homepage, understand what we do at a glance, and book the help they need. Core user flow • Visitors arrive on a polished landing page, pick a service, complete a simple booking form, choose a date and time, upload any helpful photos, and pay online and an option to pay cash. • They can just as easily request a quote when details are unclear. • Once the booking is placed, customers create (or log in to) an account where they can track job status in real time and review past orders. Back-office requirements On my side I want a clean dashboard—accessible from any device—to view, edit, and assign incoming jobs, update progress, issue invoices or refunds, and export basic reports. Staff logins with role-based permissions are essential so the team only sees what they need. Key expectations – Sleek, fast UI that feels current rather than templated. – Secure payment integration (Stripe or similar). – Responsive design and clear calls to action. – SEO-ready structure and Google Analytics connected. – Training or concise documentation so my team can add services, prices, and promotions without developer help. Tech stack is open; WordPress with custom plugins, Laravel + Vue, or a React/Node build are all fine as long as performance, security, and future scalability are covered. Deliverables 1. Live website hosted on our domain, HTTPS enabled. 2. Admin dashboard with role controls and job management. 3. Payment gateway fully tested in production mode. 4. One-page guide (or short screen-share session) explaining routine updates. If you have previous examples of service-booking portals or dashboards you built, share the links so I can gauge fit. Let’s create a site that impresses at first click and keeps operations tidy behind the scenes. Presupuesto: 2.0-8.0 USD Skills: PHP, Website Design, WordPress, MySQL, HTML, Laravel
