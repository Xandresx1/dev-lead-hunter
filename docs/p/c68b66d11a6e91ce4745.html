Record yourself using Meta Muse on your phone (US based testers only) I want to see how an AI personal assistant feels to use in real life. You must be US based and already have access to Meta Muse (US, 18+). Please do not bid if you do not have access yet. Only US based testers will be considered. To be considered, your bid must include a screenshot of your Muse home screen as proof of access. Bids without a screenshot will not be shortlisted. Record your phone screen with your voice explaining what you feel at each step. Do these tasks in Muse, in this order: Start with these three: 1. Ask Muse, out loud or by typing: "What is the wow moment for your users? Show me it." Then do whatever Muse suggests, start to finish. 2. Ask Muse: "What do people use you for the most?" Then do that task fully. 3. Ask Muse: "Save me money" and nothing else. Then let it do whatever it offers, start to finish, saying out loud what you feel. For tasks 1 to 3, say out loud as you go: what you expected, the exact moment it felt amazing if it did, how it made you feel, and whether you would tell a friend about it and why. Then do these: 4. Your very first time opening it, from sign up to the first reply. 5. Book a restaurant for Saturday. 6. Find and buy a cheap phone case (you can stop before paying). 7. Track a flight price. 8. Set a reminder. 9. Find a dentist and book, or get as close as it allows. 10. Plan a weekend trip under a budget. 11. One thing you would really use it for. For each of tasks 4 to 11, say out loud: what felt easy, what felt magic, where you got confused, and whether you trusted it was really done. At the very end, answer in one minute: "If I had to tell a friend why Muse is amazing in one sentence, I would say..." Deliver: one continuous screen recording with clear audio, from opening Muse to the end of the last task. Nothing cut. Hide your personal details, like card numbers and addresses.
