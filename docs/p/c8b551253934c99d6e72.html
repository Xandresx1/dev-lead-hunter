Sourdough Bakery Site Website Design & Development for Artisan Sourdough Bakery Project: Sourdough Clouds Bakery — Grass Valley, California We are looking for a creative, experienced web designer/developer to build a beautiful, professional, mobile-friendly website for our artisan sourdough bakery. We want something unique, warm, inviting, and easy to navigate — not a generic bakery template. Our bakery specializes in naturally fermented artisan sourdough bread, specialty loaves, focaccia, challah, cookies, and other baked goods. 1. Website Concept & Design Our website should be inspired by the journey of sourdough bread, from a living starter to a finished loaf. We have an existing watercolor-style illustration showing the stages of sourdough bread making. We want to use this illustration as inspiration for the website's overall design, colors, graphics, and navigation. The design should feel: - Artisan, handcrafted, warm, and welcoming - Natural, rustic, and elegant - Inspired by watercolor illustrations, wheat, flour, dough, and freshly baked bread - Professional, clean, and easy to navigate - Beautiful on both desktop and mobile devices We do not want complicated animations or a website that forces visitors through the bread-making process. Customers should be able to navigate directly to any section. 2. Unique Website Navigation — Very Important Instead of traditional navigation buttons such as "About Us," "Our Story," and "Products," we want the main navigation to follow the stages of sourdough bread making.
