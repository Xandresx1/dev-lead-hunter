Custom School Labelling E-commerce Platform with Scalable School Management Architecture Project: School Labelling E-commerce and Management Platform 1. Project Overview I am looking for an experienced full-stack developer or software development team to build a custom, cloud-based school labelling e-commerce platform. The initial business will allow parents to order personalised textbook and clothing labels for their children. My business will handle all production, order processing and fulfilment. The platform must include parent accounts, school and learner management, personalised product ordering, secure payments, order tracking and an internal administration and production back office. This is the first stage of a larger software ecosystem. Approximately six months after launch, I intend to introduce school library and textbook allocation functionality. Future expansion may include attendance management, learner identification, tuck shop management and cashless learner accounts. The system must be designed from the beginning to accommodate these future modules without requiring a complete redevelopment. 2. Phase 1: Initial MVP Requirements A. Parent Accounts Secure registration and login. Parent contact and address details. Multiple children linked to one account. Ability to edit child profiles. Order and payment history. Saved delivery addresses. Password recovery and notifications. Parents must be able to manage multiple children, including children attending different schools. B. School Database My business will manage the school database internally. Administrators must be able to add, edit, search and manage schools, including addresses and contact information. Parents must select their children's schools from this database when registering their children. The system must accommodate different grades and classes and support future library integration. C. Learner Profiles Each child must have an individual profile linked to their parent or guardian and school. Profiles must accommodate: Full name. School. Grade and class. Personalisation details. Parent/guardian association. Order history. Future textbook allocation records. D. Product Catalogue and Personalisation Initial products: Personalised textbook labels. Personalised clothing labels. Additional school labelling products in future. Administrators must manage products, categories, images, designs, sizes, quantities, variations and pricing. Parents must be able to select products, enter children's names, choose designs and quantities, and confirm personalisation details. Parents must be able to order for multiple children and combine different products in one shopping cart. Important: Every product must retain the correct child's name and personalisation details so that production staff can clearly distinguish products belonging to different children. E.
