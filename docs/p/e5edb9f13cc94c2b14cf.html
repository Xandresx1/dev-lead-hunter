AI Legal Marketing Automation Platform What We Want Built 1. AI Legal Content Generation Automatically research relevant NSW legal topics, legislation and legal developments. Automated Social Media Publishing Automatically distribute approved content to: Facebook Instagram LinkedIn Google Business Profile Each platform should receive appropriately formatted content. 4. AI Video Generation Automatically generate short-form legal educational video scripts. Create professional vertical videos in 9:16 format. Generate natural AI voiceovers. Include subtitles, captions, branding and calls to action. Use professional stock footage and branded templates. 5. AI Digital Twin Videos – Important Requirement We also want to create AI-generated digital-twin videos featuring a realistic AI version of our legal presenter. The digital twin should be able to deliver weekly legal tips and educational videos without requiring the presenter to physically record every video. The system should: Generate scripts based on relevant legal topics. Use our approved digital-twin likeness and voice. Generate realistic presenter-style videos. Include subtitles, branding and calls to action. Automatically prepare and publish approved videos to Facebook and YouTube. Allow videos to be downloaded and reused on other platforms. 6. Mandatory Solicitor Approval Workflow Nothing can ever be published without approval. The system must: Research and generate content. Create graphics, captions and videos. Send everything to an approval dashboard. Allow an authorised solicitor to approve, reject or request edits. Only publish content after explicit approval. Maintain a record of approvals and published content. 7. Future Reporting The system should be designed to support: Post and video performance tracking. Reach and engagement. Follower growth.
