Tour & Transfer Booking Platform I require a full-cycle booking and operational system that covers every stage of my business—from the moment a traveler clicks the "Book Now" button to the moment a driver completes the trip. At launch, the platform must provide three core experiences: • An intuitive online booking flow where customers can book private transfers, scheduled tours, or airport transfers, apply promotional codes, and make secure payments. • A staff console that allows my team to view, edit, cancel, or refund any booking, and track daily manifests in real time. • A scheduling engine that automatically matches drivers with vehicles, while also enabling drivers within a pool to accept open jobs or apply for new assignments via a simple interface. I am open to modern technology stacks such as Laravel + Vue, Node + React, or C#/.NET, provided that the code is clean, well-documented, and delivered in a Git repository with a full commit history. REST or GraphQL endpoints are essential to allow for the future addition of mobile applications. Role-based access, multilingual support, email/SMS notifications, and basic reporting must be integrated from the outset. For consideration, please submit a detailed project proposal covering the following: – Your proposed architecture and technology choices. – Milestones and timelines leading up to an initial MVP running on a test server. – Relevant past projects demonstrating your experience in handling similar booking or logistics challenges. – Your approach to data security and future scalability. Expected deliverables: 1. Complete source code and database schema. 2. Installation and testing phase on a standard dedicated server. 3. One month of bug-fixing support following the launch. If your plan demonstrates an understanding of the workflows for both passengers and operational staff, we can promptly move to the contract stage and begin development.
